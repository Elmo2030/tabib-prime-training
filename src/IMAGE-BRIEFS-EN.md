**Clinical picture**

multiple well-demarcated erythematous plaques with **thick silvery-white

### 2 · `case-03-ambiguous` — slide 28 — **the hardest and most important**

**Clinical picture**
An annular or well-demarcated erythematous plaque with **peripheral scale**,
on the lower leg, in a patient with **Fitzpatrick V–VI (dark) skin**.
Ideally a lesion that has been treated with a topical corticosteroid, so the
appearance is altered — *tinea incognito* is the classic example.

**Must show**
**Dark skin. Non-negotiable.** The teaching point of this case is that
erythema is harder to assess on darker skin, and the caption on the slide
says exactly that. A light-skinned image would contradict the slide in front
of the audience.

**Search**
```
site:dermnetnz.org tinea corporis skin of colour
site:dermnetnz.org tinea incognito
tinea corporis dark skin Fitzpatrick V annular plaque
skin of color dermatology atlas annular scaly plaque leg
```
Also worth trying: **Skin of Color Society** image library, and
**VisualDx**'s skin-of-colour collection if your trial is active.

**If you cannot find one** — tell me. I will rewrite the case around a skin
tone you *can* source, rather than let the slide claim one thing while the
image shows another. The lesson changes but stays honest.

### 3 · `case-final-scalp` — slide 59

**Clinical picture**
A scalp patch with **localised hair loss** and light scaling, relatively
well-demarcated. Adult male, Fitzpatrick IV. Tinea capitis is the usual
labelled example.

**Must show**
The localised hair loss. That is the axis of the case — scaling alone,
without visible hair loss, does not carry it.

**Search**
```
site:dermnetnz.org tinea capitis adult
tinea capitis scalp scaling patchy alopecia
scalp psoriasis versus tinea capitis clinical photograph
```

## What I do when a file arrives

1. Resize and compress — **no crop**, watermark intact
2. Add the credit line on the slide: *source — DermNet — link*
3. Remove the "illustrative image, not a real patient" label
4. Record licence and attribution in `img/CREDITS.md`
5. Full QA and deploy

One image at a time is fine. Each one that lands gets installed.
